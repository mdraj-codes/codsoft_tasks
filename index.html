01 — UNDERSTAND THE THREAT

What is Phishing?

🎣

Phishing

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals use fake messages, emails or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

🔑

What Do Attackers Want?

Attackers may try to steal passwords, banking information, OTPs, personal information or account credentials.

⚠️

Why Is It Dangerous?

A single careless click can expose your account, personal data or financial information.

02 — CHECK BEFORE YOU CLICK

How to Spot a Suspicious Email

⚠️ URGENT: Your Account Will Be Closed!

Dear Customer,

Your account has been suspended. Click the link below immediately to verify your account.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • Unexpected or urgent messages
  • Threats such as "your account will close"
  • Unknown sender
  • Suspicious links
  • Requests for passwords or OTPs
  • Spelling or grammar mistakes

✅ What You Should Do

  • Do not click suspicious links
  • Verify the sender
  • Visit the official website manually
  • Never share your OTP or password
  • Report suspicious messages
  • Delete the phishing message

03 — VERIFY THE WEBSITE

Fake Login Pages & Websites

Sign in to your account

🔍 Before entering your password, check:

  • Is the domain name correct?
  • Does the website use HTTPS?
  • Did you reach the website from an unexpected link?
  • Does the website look unusual or poorly designed?
  • Is the domain pretending to be a trusted company?

04 — KNOW THE MANIPULATION

Common Social Engineering Techniques

01

Urgency

Attackers create panic by saying you must act immediately.

02

Fear

Criminals may threaten account suspension, penalties or legal consequences.

03

Authority

Attackers may pretend to be banks, companies, teachers, managers or government officials.

04

Reward

Fake prizes, discounts and job offers can be used to attract victims.

05 — LEARN FROM EXAMPLES

Real-World Phishing Scenario

📧 The Fake Bank Alert

Imagine receiving a message claiming that your bank account will be blocked unless you verify your details immediately.

The message contains a link that opens a login page. The page looks similar to your bank's website, but the address is actually controlled by an attacker.

The safe response:

Do not use the provided link. Open your bank's official website or application yourself and check your account.

06 — STAY PROTECTED

Phishing Safety Checklist

🔐 Use strong, unique passwords
🔑 Enable multi-factor authentication
🚫 Never share OTPs or passwords
🔗 Avoid unknown links
🔍 Check website addresses carefully
📱 Keep software and browsers updated
📧 Verify unexpected emails
🚨 Report suspicious messages
